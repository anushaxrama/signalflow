04 / SIGNAL WATCH

A closer look at change.

3σ
About these signals

Observed values are compared with up to 48 preceding intervals; at least 12 actuals are required. A 10 gCO₂/kWh standard-deviation floor limits noisy alerts. Statistical flags are signals for investigation, not confirmed incidents.

A NOTE ON THE DATA

Real signals.
Clear boundaries.

SignalFlow reads national carbon intensity and generation mix from the NESO Carbon Intensity API for Great Britain. Data is published in 30-minute intervals; observed actuals can arrive later and be revised.

Renewables here means wind, solar and hydro. Forecasts stay separate from observations. The workload lab compares historical windows and estimates operational emissions—it does not predict future savings.
